About FitLink

Finding clothes that fit shouldn’t feel like a guessing game.

We’re FitLink, a Virtual Enterprises firm working on a simpler way to find clothing and gear. Our idea is straightforward: combine a camera-guided scan preview, your style preferences, and comfort needs to help narrow down the options.

Maybe you’re looking for everyday clothes, sports gear, or insoles for sore feet. FitLink is designed to help you make a more informed choice, instead of guessing from a size label alone.
